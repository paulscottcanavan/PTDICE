Pale Tides Dice Roller

Experimental dice roller for internal testing

Dice Rules

• Match or beat the Task Difficulty to succeed at the task!
• By default, rolls of 4+ are successful.
• If you have advantage, rolls of 3+ are successful.
• If you have disadvantage, rolls of 5+ are successful.
• 6's explode! Another dice is rolled automatically.

• Rolling 3×6 results in a Boon!
• Rolling 3×1 results in a Bane!
• If you get a Boon and Bane, they cancel each other out.
